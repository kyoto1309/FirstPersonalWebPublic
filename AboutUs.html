What is GSC


GSC (Game Showcase Central) is a platform dedicated to helping independent game developers showcase their creations and reach a wider audience.

Our website gives smaller and lesser-known games an opportunity to gain recognition. Whether you're an independent developer looking to promote your game or a player searching for something new to play, GSC has something for you.

Visitors can explore different games, view screenshots and videos, and learn more about each game's features and details.
